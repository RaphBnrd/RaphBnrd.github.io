This is a simple Speech-to-Text application using [VOSK](https://alphacephei.com/vosk/) for offline speech recognition. It can transcribe audio from your microphone in real-time and send the transcription to various destinations such as keyboard input, console output, or a text file.

It requires Python 3.12+ and works on Windows, Linux, and MacOS.
